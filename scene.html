One thing this sample raises

With two other characters on screen, Michael and Anna are the same wine. The name tells them apart, the colour does not. In a two-hander that never came up; in a crowd scene it might.

I have deliberately left it alone rather than inventing a third colour, because wine-means-everyone-else is the rule the whole app leans on. Worth a look on the phone before we decide it needs solving.
