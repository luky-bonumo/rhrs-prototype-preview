Screen by screen

What each one is for

The same list as the map, in reading order, with a note on what the screen has to do.

Dashboard Landing screen. Last opened scripts, shortcuts, and the week's stats. The only screen on a wine background. Drawn
All scripts Every play, with scene counts and whether you have a part in it. Drawn
Script detail The scenes of one play, plus a whole-play entry. Leads to Edit play. Drawn
Edit play Characters and their line counts, and reorder, edit or delete scenes. Drawn
Read Plain reading. Lines separated by rules, private notes and stage directions, no playback. Drawn
Listen The scene plays itself. Lines become cards, the one being read is outlined, and the transport takes the colour of whoever is speaking. Drawn
Rehearse Cue and answer. Someone else's line plays as audio in wine; your own line records in coral with the text hidden. Drawn
Evaluation What the app heard you say, against the line as written. Opens from Rehearse, closes back to it. Drawn
Line menu Per-line actions: start here in Listen or Rehearse, edit the line, add a private note, record audio. Sheet
Mode settings Settings for the mode you are in — text size, which lines show, note defaults, playback speed. Sheet
Note editor Writing a private note or a stage direction on a line. Reachable from the line menu, not yet drawn. To design
Self tape Recording an audition to camera. Shortcut exists on the dashboard, screen not yet drawn. To design
My characters The roles you play across all scripts. Shortcut exists on the dashboard, screen not yet drawn. To design
Global settings Account, voices, rehearsal defaults, notifications, storage, appearance, subscription. To design
